# Studymate — Cambridge O Level Study App

A mobile-first (460 px max-width) single-file web study app for **Cambridge O Level**, built with vanilla HTML, CSS, STIX Two Text mathematical typography, and 2D Canvas interactive tools.

## What's Included (All 9 Subjects Live)
- **3-Level Home Navigation:** `Home (All 9 Subjects)` → `Subject (Chapters & Topics)` → `Topic (7 Sections: Objectives, Learn, Command Words, Practice, Explore/Lab, Real Life, Progress)`
- **Mathematical Typography Engine:** Full STIX Two Text notation for powers, roots with vinculum bars, stacked fractions, subscripts, and nuclear nuclide symbols across Maths and Physics.
- **9 Live Cambridge O Level Subjects (95 dedicated topic pages · 29 interactive 2D Canvas tools · 51 real-life WebP photos):**
  1. **Physics (5054)** — 6 chapters, 21 topic pages, 15 Canvas simulations, Paper 1 MCQs, Paper 2 self-marked structured questions, Paper 3/4 virtual practicals, Roman + Nastaliq Urdu notes, and 35 photos (`images/photo-01.webp`–`photo-35.webp`).
  2. **Mathematics — Syllabus D (4024)** — 9 sections, 16 topic pages, 4 Canvas tools, Paper 1 & Paper 2 practice, and 16 photos (`images/photo-36.webp`–`photo-51.webp`).
  3. **Computer Science (2210)** — Paper 1 Systems & Paper 2 Algorithms/Logic, 10 topic pages, and 3 Canvas tools (Binary/Hex, CPU Fetch-Decode-Execute, Logic Gates).
  4. **Business Studies (7115)** — 6 sections, 12 topic pages, and 2 Canvas tools (Break-Even Chart & Financial Ratios).
  5. **English Language (1123)** — Paper 1 Reading & Paper 2 Writing, 6 topic pages, and 1 Canvas Writing Planner.
  6. **Urdu — Second Language (3248)** — Paper 1 Reading & Writing & Paper 2 Grammar, Writing & Translation, 8 topic pages, and 1 Canvas Urdu Planner.
  7. **Islamiyat (2058)** — Paper 1 (Qur'an & Seerah) & Paper 2 (Hadith, Caliphs & Pillars), 8 topic pages, and 1 Canvas Seerah & Caliphate Timeline.
  8. **Pakistan Studies (2059)** — Paper 1 History (1707–1999) & Paper 2 Environment of Pakistan, 7 topic pages, and 1 Canvas History & Indus Explorer.
  9. **Global Perspectives (2069)** — Component 1 Written Exam, Component 2 Individual Report (1500–2000 words), & Component 3 Team Project + Reflective Paper (750–1000 words), 7 topic pages, and 1 Canvas Component & Source Evaluator.

## Updating Your GitHub Repository
1. Copy `index.html`, `.nojekyll`, and `README.md` from this folder into the root of your existing GitHub repository (replacing the older `index.html`).
2. Keep your existing `images/` folder (`photo-01.webp` through `photo-51.webp`) in place alongside `index.html` (no video or audio files are needed).
3. Commit and push to `main` — GitHub Pages will publish all 9 live subjects automatically.
